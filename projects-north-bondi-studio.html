SELECTED PROJECT

North Bondi Studio

A clever 30sqm micro-apartment renovation tripling storage space and introducing a layered, moody, and deeply personal beachside aesthetic.

The Design Story

Faced with a highly restricted 30sqm footprint, Patina Design Studio was tasked with reconfiguring this micro-apartment to create a highly flexible, functional home.

The client's brief was to pack the tiny space with personality, warmth, and items they genuinely loved, avoiding a sterile or minimalist "hotel" look. Our response was a design that embraces a "moody and homey" aesthetic. Rather than using bright whites, we selected a palette of deep blues, textured linen drapery, and rich timbers, pulling subtle elements from the studio's Bondi Beach location without leaning on literal coastal clichés.

The layout was completely reconfigured. By designing highly tailored custom joinery, we successfully tripled the original storage capacity. Integrated bed structures, pull-out wardrobes, and multi-functional desk-dining setups create distinct zones within a single open space, allowing the client to live, work, sleep, and entertain with absolute ease.

Micro-Living Optimization

Bespoke Detailing: Every single millimeter of this 30sqm studio was measured and drawn with precision. The custom joinery hides a complete home office, wardrobe, and media center, folding away effortlessly when not in use.

The Outcome: A highly resolved, tactile, and expansive micro-apartment that proves premium luxury is defined by careful consideration, not square footage.
