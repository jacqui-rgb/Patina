SELECTED PROJECT

Dover Heights Duplex

A striking new-build residential duplex maximizing space, light, and value on a highly narrow Sydney coastal site.

The Design Story

Beginning as a Layout Assessment to evaluate off-plan opportunities, this project grew into a Full Service Design engagement to deliver a pair of highly distinctive, premium duplexes.

The developer's directive was clear: create two unique and luxury dwellings that stood out from standard neighborhood developments to attract premium buyers. To achieve this on a narrow footprint, we shifted the focus to architectural volume, circulation flow, and highly integrated bespoke joinery that acts as functional sculpture.

By manipulating the wall configurations, refining stair placements, and raising ceiling heights, the spaces feel expansive and fluid rather than restricted. We integrated premium elements such as a custom-detailed marble island, a walk-in pantry, a bespoke cocktail bar, and an innovative drop-down TV system hidden in the living ceiling. The materials combine coastal sophistication with urban edge, featuring rich timbers, soft travertines, and black metal details.

Commercial Success & Detailing

A Commercial Milestone: Upon completion, Patina Design Studio collaborated directly with the marketing real estate agents to present and highlight the bespoke design choices to prospective buyers, contributing to a record-setting sale price for both duplex residences.

Bespoke Elements: Features hand-detailed built-in bars, integrated media fireplaces, custom wardrobes, vanity detailing, and an ultra-functional concealed laundry.
