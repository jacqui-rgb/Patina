What Happens Next?

Once your enquiry has been submitted, we will review the information provided and contact you to arrange a complimentary introductory call.

This initial conversation allows us to understand the nature of your project, answer process-related questions and determine whether Patina Design Studio is the right fit.

Project-specific design advice, layout feedback, concepts, sourcing and recommendations are provided through a paid Design Consultation, rather than during the introductory call.

Where there is clear alignment, the next step is a paid Design Consultation. This advisory session is designed to clarify your objectives, assess the opportunities and constraints of your property, and determine the most appropriate path forward.

Design Consultations are $1,200 inc GST.

We look forward to learning more about your project and discussing the most appropriate path forward.
