Can we engage you to review a property before we purchase it?

Yes. A Pre-Purchase Design Review can help you understand a property’s spatial potential, limitations and renovation opportunities before making a major commitment. Patina Design Studio looks beyond cosmetic finishes to assess layout, flow, natural light, storage, structural constraints and whether the property can realistically support the way you want to live.

Related Pages: Pre-Purchase Design Review • Related Projects: Off-Plan Penthouse Review

Is a Pre-Purchase Design Review different from a building inspection?

Yes. A building inspection assesses defects, moisture, pests, compliance and structural concerns. A Pre-Purchase Design Review is different. It focuses on spatial potential, layout functionality, natural light, storage, renovation opportunity and whether the property can support your lifestyle and long-term plans.

Related Pages: Pre-Purchase Design Review

Does Patina Design Studio take the place of a buyer’s agent?

No. Patina Design Studio does not take the place of a buyer’s agent. A buyer’s agent assists with property sourcing, value assessment and purchase negotiation. Patina Design Studio assesses the property from a design perspective, including layout, spatial potential, natural light, constraints, renovation opportunities and long-term liveability.

Design Philosophy

Why is spatial planning so important?

Spatial planning determines how rooms relate, how people move, where furniture sits, what can be stored, how privacy is maintained and whether daily routines feel intuitive. These decisions shape the experience of the home before materials or decorative selections are introduced. Finishes and furniture cannot compensate for a floor plan that does not support the way the home needs to be used.

Related Pages: Layout Assessment • Related Reading: Journal No.2 | Why a Perfect Floor Plan Can Still Feel Wrong

How do you approach designing for everyday living?

We begin by understanding how the home needs to support the people living in it. This includes routines, storage, movement, privacy, maintenance and the practical moments that determine whether a home feels easy to use. Planning, detailing, materials, lighting and furnishing are then developed as one interior rather than as separate room-by-room decisions.

Related Pages: Full Service Design • Related Reading: Journal No.4 | The Small Things You Tolerate

What makes a home feel complete?

A home feels complete when its floor plan, room relationships, joinery, lighting, materials and furniture appear to belong to the same line of thought. Each decision supports the next rather than reading as a series of separate architectural, interior and furnishing layers.

Related Pages: Full Service Design • Related Projects: Vaucluse Residence

What is The Invisible Layer Series?

The Invisible Layer Series is Patina Design Studio’s newsletter exploring the thinking, planning, coordination and detail behind complete residential interiors. It examines the less visible decisions that shape how a home functions, feels and is experienced. Selected editions are also published in the Journal.

Related Pages: Journal

Do you follow trends?

We are more interested in longevity than trends. Each project is shaped around the client, the property, the architecture and the way the home needs to function. The aim is to create a home that feels refined, personal and enduring rather than tied to a passing style.

Our Process

What does the process of working with Patina Design Studio look like?

Patina Design Studio guides projects through four client-facing stages: Understand, Define, Design and Deliver. Behind these stages sits a detailed framework covering consultation, briefing, spatial planning, design development, documentation, tender coordination, construction-stage design management, procurement, installation and handover. The brief, spatial planning, design direction, documentation, construction coordination, furnishing, installation and handover are developed in sequence, with the right decisions made before the project moves too far ahead.

When is the best time to engage an interior designer or interior architect?

Ideally, before the architect and wider project team are appointed. This allows Patina Design Studio to establish the brief and interior spatial direction first, then recommend and coordinate the consultants required to support it. Where a team or plans already exist, the studio should be engaged while floor planning, lighting, joinery, circulation, storage and furniture requirements can still be influenced.

Working Together & Collaboration

Can you work with our architect or builder?

Yes. The ideal structure is for Patina Design Studio to be appointed first as the lead design consultant. This gives the client one clear brief and spatial direction before the wider project team begins. The studio then recommends and coordinates the architect, builder, engineers and specialist consultants best suited to the project. Where an architect, builder or consultant has already been appointed, Patina Design Studio can work with the existing team. Roles, responsibilities and decision-making need to be clearly established so the interior direction can be developed and carried through the project.

Who will be working on my project?

Each project is led by the founder and principal of Patina Design Studio, who establishes the brief and design direction and remains closely involved throughout the process. On Full Service Design projects, the studio may also assemble and coordinate the architect, builder, engineers and specialist consultants required, alongside the studio team, suppliers, trades and specialist collaborators. Patina Design Studio accepts a limited number of projects so this level of principal involvement can be maintained.

Do you work on projects outside Sydney?

Yes. Patina Design Studio is based in Sydney and undertakes selected interstate and international projects where the scope, location and level of involvement are suitable. Projects outside Sydney may require travel time, site visits and additional coordination costs, which are discussed before engagement.

Can you work remotely with clients?

Patina Design Studio does not offer remote-only design services. For selected interstate or international projects, parts of the process may be handled remotely, but this is supported by planned site visits, in-person reviews, material assessment and proper project coordination.

How does Patina Design Studio handle project photography, publication, and privacy?

Completed project photography is an important part of Patina Design Studio’s portfolio and media presence, and is discussed with clients during the project process.

We understand that many clients value discretion. Patina Design Studio does not publish client names, exact addresses, personal details, or private circumstances without permission. Where a project is photographed, published, submitted for awards, or shared online, the way the project is identified and described is handled with care.
