Frequently Asked Questions

Clear guidance on working together, our design philosophy, and the path to realizing your bespoke residential project.

Services & Projects

What services does Patina Design Studio offer?

Patina Design Studio is a full-service interior architecture and design practice specialising in high-end residential projects. Our services include spatial planning, interior architecture, interior design, construction documentation packages, procurement, design project management, custom furniture selection, styling, and final installation coordination. We work closely with clients, architects, consultants, and builders from early planning through to final completion.

What types of projects do you work on?

We specialise in high-end residential projects including apartment renovations, complete house renovations, new builds, developments, furnishing projects, and luxury holiday homes. We also take on select hospitality (boutique hotels, bars, restaurants), premium retail, and workplace projects depending on the brief, scope, and alignment of design values.

Who do you work with?

We work with homeowners, developers, and private clients seeking highly considered, premium residential spaces. We often collaborate closely with architects, builders, consultants, trades, and broader project teams throughout both the design and construction phases.

Do you work on off-plan apartments?

Yes. We can assist with off-plan apartments, particularly when clients want to review layouts, finishes, joinery details, lighting designs, furniture planning, and developer upgrade opportunities before key architectural decisions are locked in.

Do you only work on luxury homes?

We work on highly considered residential projects where clients value thoughtful planning, refined detailing, and a collaborative design process. The scale of a project is often less important than the level of care, detail, and coordination required to achieve a beautiful, custom outcome.

Do you offer furnishing and styling only?

Yes, depending on the project. Some clients engage the studio specifically for furnishing, styling, and custom procurement independently of larger renovation or construction works, helping to elevate existing spatial envelopes.

Do you take on projects with existing furniture or pieces clients already own?

Yes. Where appropriate, we can incorporate existing high-quality furniture, artwork, or sentimental pieces into the broader design direction. This creates a home that feels deeply personal, layered, and reflective of the client's life and travels.

Do you work on existing structures or only new builds?

We work on both. Our projects range from complex renovations and interior reconfigurations of existing structures to knock-down rebuilds and luxury new developments.

Planning & Process

When is the best time to engage an interior designer or interior architect?

The earlier, the better. Many important design decisions—such as room layouts, lighting plans, and integrated joinery pathways—happen well before finishes and furniture selections begin. Early involvement allows these elements to be resolved before they become difficult, restrictive, or costly to change.

Why does early planning matter so much?

Spatial planning, lighting, circulation, storage, and custom joinery all heavily influence how a home functions and feels. The earlier these invisible structural layers are considered, the more cohesive, effortless, and elegant the final physical outcome tends to be.

Can you help before we purchase a property?

Yes. We often assist during the pre-purchase phase to help assess the potential, spatial functionality, and interior opportunities within a property before major capital commitments are made.

What if we do not know exactly what we want yet?

That is completely normal. Part of our expert role is helping clients clarify priorities, identify opportunities, and shape a cohesive aesthetic direction and functional brief through our design process.

Can you help balance budget and design priorities?

Yes. We help clients prioritize where their design investment will have the greatest impact on how the home feels, functions, and performs long-term, ensuring resources are allocated thoughtfully.

Collaboration & Logistics

Can you work with our architect or builder?

Yes. We have a trusted network of preferred partners and builders we can recommend, but we are also happy to work alongside a team you have already engaged. We regularly collaborate with external architects, builders, consultants, and specialty trades.

Who will be working on my project?

Every single project is directly led and conceptualized by founder Jacqui Hurwitz. Depending on the scope, the team may include designers, consultants, suppliers, and skilled trades coordinated by the studio. We intentionally take on a select number of projects each year to ensure high-level, dedicated attention.

Do you attend site meetings?

Yes. Site meetings are an important part of our process to maintain close alignment between the design vision, construction documentation, and the actual build process, ensuring quality assurance.

Do you work on projects outside Sydney?

Yes. We work across Sydney, interstate, and internationally. Projects located more than 15km from our studio may incur additional travel time and coordination costs, which are discussed transparently and agreed upon prior to engagement.

Can you work remotely with clients?

Yes. Depending on the scope and location, parts of the design process can be managed remotely alongside strategically scheduled site visits, material deliveries, and consultant coordination meetings.
