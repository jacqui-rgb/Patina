Frequently Asked Questions

Clear guidance on working together, our design philosophy, and the path to realizing your bespoke residential project.

Working Together

What services does Patina Design Studio offer?

We provide comprehensive, full-service interior architecture and design. Our capabilities span spatial planning and layout optimization, detailed construction documentation, material and finish curation, custom joinery and furniture design, decorative lighting layouts, furniture procurement, and final installation styling.

What types of projects do you work on?

Our focus is on highly resolved, high-end residential projects including new builds, significant renovations, duplex developments, apartments, and penthouses. We also undertake select hospitality, boutique commercial, and premium retail spaces that demand highly considered, bespoke aesthetics.

When is the best time to engage Patina Design Studio?

The earlier, the better. Engaging our studio during the early planning stages—often before builders are appointed or architectural designs are finalized—gives us the freedom to perfect the spatial layout, maximize natural light, integrate joinery seamlessly, and resolve interior flow before construction limits arise.

Can you work with our existing architect or builder?

Absolutely. We highly value collaboration and regularly partner with external architects, builders, project managers, and landscape designers to ensure a unified, cohesive execution of both the interior and exterior elements of your property.

Do you work on projects outside of Sydney?

Yes, while our core practice is based in Sydney, Australia, we design and manage residential projects for clients interstate and internationally. We are fully equipped for remote collaboration and travel as required to oversee key site milestones.

Who will be leading my project?

Every single project undertaken by Patina Design Studio is directly led and conceptualized by founder Jacqui Hurwitz, ensuring that our signature considered, highly detailed philosophy and standard of quality is maintained from design through to final handover.
