OUR SERVICES

Full Service Design

As the studio's most comprehensive offering, Full Service Design establishes the strategic, architectural and interior direction of a project from early planning through to completion.

Patina Design Studio is frequently engaged during the formative stages of a project, when the greatest opportunities still exist to influence functionality, spatial planning and the overall outcome. Working alongside architects, builders and specialist consultants, we help shape the key decisions that establish the foundation of the home before constraints are introduced and opportunities are lost.

Our Approach

Full Service Design is intended for significant new builds, substantial renovations and complete residential redesigns requiring a coordinated approach from early planning through to completion.

Acting as the lead interior design consultant, Patina Design Studio works across the many layers of a project including spatial planning, interior architecture, joinery, lighting, materials, furnishings and consultant coordination to ensure decisions are considered as part of a cohesive whole rather than in isolation.

We are often engaged during the formative stages of a project, helping establish the project brief, refine floor plans, identify opportunities and shape the design direction before key decisions become fixed.

As the project progresses, we remain closely involved through design development, documentation, procurement and construction, coordinating with the wider project team to protect the integrity of the design through to completion.

What's Included

  • Spatial planning & floor layouts
  • Interior architecture & design
  • Bespoke joinery & specialised space design
  • Finishes, materials & colour palettes
  • Lighting, hardware & fixture specification
  • Furniture & art curation
  • Technical documentation for pricing & coordination
  • On-site client representation
  • Sourcing & procurement
  • Turn-key delivery & installation coordination

When Full Service Design Creates the Greatest Value

Functionality, architecture, interiors and consultant coordination are most effective when resolved as part of a single design strategy. The most successful homes are the result of many interconnected decisions working together as a cohesive whole.

Greater Influence Over The Outcome

The greatest opportunities to refine layouts, maximise volume and improve functionality exist before structural decisions are finalised.

Better Team Coordination

Early collaboration between architects, builders, engineers and specialist consultants helps ensure the project is aligned from the outset.

Protecting Design Intent

Detailed documentation and ongoing involvement help maintain the integrity of the design throughout construction.

Greater Design Cohesion

Considering spatial planning, joinery, lighting, materials and furnishings together creates a home that feels cohesive, considered and complete.

Who It's For

Homeowners and developers embarking on significant new builds, substantial renovations, or major additions. It is for those who value precise spatial planning, architectural depth, and a calm, guided journey from concept to completion.

STRATEGIC ROLE

Why Clients Engage Patina Design Studio Early

We act as the lead design consultant, establishing the creative and spatial foundation of the home before other partners are appointed or decisions are locked in.

LEAD CONSULTANT

Patina Design Studio is frequently the first consultant engaged, helping establish the project brief, direction, and design principles before other appointments are made.

TEAM ASSEMBLY

The studio assists in identifying and coordinating the consultant team, including architects, builders, engineers, and specialist consultants.

DEFINING THE FOUNDATION

Spatial planning, functionality, interior architecture, and key design opportunities are resolved before constraints are introduced.

GUARDING THE DESIGN INTENT

Documentation, construction involvement, and consultant coordination ensure the original design intent is protected through delivery.

RESOLVING DECISIONS EARLIER

It is far easier to refine a home on paper than after walls, services and structure have been built. Early planning allows critical decisions to be resolved when change is still simple, efficient and cost-effective.
