OUR SERVICES

Full Service Design

Full Service Design is the studio’s most comprehensive engagement for substantial renovations, new builds and complete residential redesigns.

Patina Design Studio is engaged while the planning and interior direction can still be influenced. The studio examines floor plans, room relationships, circulation, joinery, lighting, materials and furniture together, then carries that connected direction through documentation, consultant coordination, construction, procurement, installation and handover.

Our Approach

Full Service Design is intended for new builds, substantial renovations and complete residential redesigns requiring continuity from early planning through completion.

An architect or building designer may establish the broader building response, but the lived interior requires concentrated attention at a different scale. Patina Design Studio focuses on how rooms connect, how people move through the home, where furniture will sit, how storage and joinery operate, how lighting supports each space and how materials transition across the interior.

Existing plans are reviewed critically rather than treated as fixed. Where necessary, Patina Design Studio refines the spatial planning and works alongside the architect, builder and specialist consultants to align the interior with structural, technical and construction requirements.

The same direction is then developed through detailed interior architecture, documentation, finishes, fixtures and furnishing. Construction-stage involvement helps protect that direction as site conditions, pricing decisions and technical questions arise, while procurement and installation complete the home as one interior.

Best suited to

Homeowners and developers undertaking new builds, substantial renovations or major additions who want one clear design direction established before the wider project team begins. It is particularly suited to clients who have not yet appointed an architect or consultant team and want those appointments shaped around the requirements of the home.

What’s Included

  • Project briefing and interior spatial strategy
  • Review and refinement of floor plans and room relationships
  • Interior architecture, circulation and furniture planning
  • Bespoke joinery, kitchens, bathrooms, wardrobes and specialised spaces
  • Lighting, finishes, materials, fixtures and hardware
  • Furniture, rugs, artwork, custom pieces design/sourcing and procurement
  • Detailed interior documentation and specifications for pricing and coordination
  • Coordination with architects, builders and specialist consultants
  • Design management during construction
  • Installation, final placement and handover (turn-key installation)
