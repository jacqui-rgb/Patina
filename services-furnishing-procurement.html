Furnishing & Procurement

Furniture, lighting, artwork and custom pieces selected, procured and installed through a complete furnishing service.

Our Approach

Every furnishing decision is considered in relation to the architecture, scale and lifestyle requirements of the home.

The process begins by understanding how the home should function, feel and be experienced before selections are made. Furniture, lighting, rugs, artwork and custom pieces are then selected to create a cohesive environment that feels personal, balanced and connected to the space.

Patina Design Studio manages sourcing, supplier coordination, procurement, freight, delivery and installation, allowing every furnishing decision to be considered within the context of the overall home.

What's Included

  • Furniture layouts & spatial planning
  • Procurement strategy & spending allocation
  • Furniture selections
  • Lighting selections
  • Rugs, artwork & accessories
  • Bespoke furniture design
  • Window treatments & drapery selections
  • Supplier liaison & procurement
  • Freight, delivery & installation coordination
  • Final styling & handover

When Furnishing & Procurement Creates the Greatest Value

Furnishing decisions made collectively create stronger outcomes than those made piece by piece over time.

FURNISHING AN ENTIRE HOME

When multiple rooms need to be furnished simultaneously, considering furniture, lighting, artwork and accessories together creates a more cohesive and balanced outcome than making selections room by room.

CUSTOM OR BESPOKE PIECES ARE REQUIRED

Many of the most successful furnishing outcomes involve custom furniture, upholstery, rugs or artwork. Early planning allows these pieces to be designed, manufactured and delivered within the broader installation schedule.

A HOME THAT FEELS COLLECTED, NOT PURCHASED

The most successful furnishing outcomes rarely come from selecting everything from a single supplier or range. By carefully curating pieces from multiple sources, we create homes that feel layered, personal and reflective of the people who live within them while maintaining a cohesive overall aesthetic.

A COORDINATED INSTALLATION

Large furnishing projects can involve months of production, shipping and deliveries. By coordinating procurement and storage in advance, we are able to install and style the home within a concentrated installation period rather than having pieces arrive gradually over time, reducing disruption and allowing furniture, rugs, lighting and artwork to be positioned as part of a complete environment rather than continually rearranged as additional items arrive.

Who It's For

Furnishing & Procurement is most often engaged by homeowners moving into a new home, completing a renovation or replacing existing furnishings.

It is suited to clients who want a considered furnishing outcome and prefer a single point of coordination across sourcing, procurement, delivery and installation.

PROCUREMENT TIMING

Planning Ahead

We recommend engaging Patina Design Studio approximately 8–12 months before your desired installation date, particularly where custom furniture, international suppliers or significant procurement is involved.

Custom pieces can require 2–6 months or more for manufacture, while international shipping can take 2–4 months before customs clearance and local delivery.
