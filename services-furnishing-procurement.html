OUR SERVICES

Furnishing & Procurement

Furniture, lighting, artwork and custom pieces selected, procured and installed through a complete furnishing service.

Our Approach

Furniture, lighting, rugs, artwork and custom pieces are planned, selected, procured and installed in relation to the architecture, room proportions and the way each area is used. The furnished areas feel settled, comfortable, well scaled and ready to live in from installation.

We begin by reviewing the architecture, room proportions, daily routines and any existing pieces that should remain. Furniture plans are developed before selections are finalised so each item is assessed in relation to movement, spacing, conversation, views and the surrounding interior.

The full scheme is then presented as one scope, giving clients a clear understanding of the design direction, budget, lead times and installation requirements before orders are placed. We manages supplier liaison, procurement, freight, delivery, installation and final placement so the client is not left coordinating separate orders or assembling the finished home over time.

Where custom pieces, multiple or international suppliers, or larger procurement are involved, early planning allows orders, lead times and installation to be coordinated with the broader project schedule.

Best suited to

Homeowners furnishing an entire home, a full level or a connected group of rooms, with planning, sourcing, procurement, delivery and installation managed together rather than as separate purchases or styling assistance.

What’s Included

  • Furniture planning and room-by-room furnishing strategy
  • Furniture, decorative lighting, rugs, artwork and accessories
  • Custom furniture and individually designed pieces
  • Window treatments and drapery
  • Integration of suitable existing furniture and artwork
  • Supplier liaison, ordering and procurement administration
  • Freight, delivery and installation coordination
  • Final placement, styling and handover
