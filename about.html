About the Studio

A practice dedicated to creating functional, perfectly proportioned, and long-term liveable homes designed with rigorous attention to detail.

Jacqui Hurwitz

Founder & Principal

Before founding Patina Design Studio, Jacqui Hurwitz spent over a decade working across complex hospitality, retail, commercial and luxury residential projects throughout Australia, London, Miami and Toronto. Her background designing high-performance commercial and hospitality environments, alongside ultra-high-end private residences, instils a rigorous, process-driven discipline into residential projects. This ensures that spatial layouts, custom joinery, and material selections are not just visually resolved, but highly functional and capable of supporting daily routines.

Throughout her career, Jacqui developed a strong belief that successful homes are shaped long before finishes, furnishings or styling are considered. The studio is often most valuable when engaged early, either prior to property purchase or at the very commencement of a renovation or new build. This early involvement allows the studio to collaborate closely with builders, engineers, certifiers, and architects, acting as a dedicated design manager. By focusing on spatial planning, layout efficiency, and interior detailing from the outset, we help resolve structural and layout conflicts on paper before construction begins, saving time and preventing costly revisions on site.

Today, that philosophy continues to guide the studio's work. We serve clients who value spatial intelligence, clear communication, and considered execution over transient trends. We intentionally limit our project intake each year to ensure every home receives our rigorous, personal involvement from initial concept through to final hand-over.

Spatially Intelligent. Thoughtfully Lived.

Our Philosophy

A Commitment to Depth

We intentionally limit our intake to a select number of projects each year, allowing us to remain closely involved throughout every stage of the process. From early planning through to final styling, we work collaboratively with clients, architects, builders and artisans to ensure each project is approached with clarity, consistency and attention to detail.

Function Before Finish

We believe the success of a home is determined long before finishes and furnishings are selected. Functionality, spatial planning and proportion form the foundation of every project, influencing how a home feels to live in both immediately and over time.

A Collaborative Partnership

Building or renovating a home is a significant undertaking requiring thoughtful coordination across many moving parts. We remain actively involved throughout design and execution, working closely with consultants, engineers, builders and suppliers to ensure the integrity of the project is maintained through to completion.
