About the Studio

A practice dedicated to creating functional, perfectly proportioned, and long-term liveable homes designed with rigorous attention to detail.

Founder & Principal

Jacqui Hurwitz

Before founding Patina Design Studio, Jacqui Hurwitz spent over a decade working across luxury residential and hospitality projects throughout Australia, London, Miami and Toronto. This international experience continues to inform the studio’s approach to proportion, materiality, functionality and the way spaces are experienced day to day.

Patina Design Studio creates highly resolved residential interiors that balance refined aesthetics with long-term liveability. Alongside the visual outcome, careful attention is given to the planning, detailing and execution that shape how a home ultimately functions and feels over time.

Jacqui remains closely involved throughout each stage of the process, from early planning and design development through to procurement, installation and final styling, ensuring each project is carried through with clarity and consistency from concept to completion.

By undertaking a select number of projects each year, the studio is able to maintain a highly involved and considered approach across every project.

Our Philosophy

Highly Resolved. Thoughtfully Lived.

A Commitment to Depth

We intentionally limit our intake to a select number of projects each year, allowing us to remain closely involved throughout every stage of the process. From early planning through to final styling, we work collaboratively with clients, architects, builders and artisans to ensure each project is approached with clarity, consistency and attention to detail.

Function, Proportion, & Longevity

We believe successful design prioritises how a space functions and feels over temporary trends. Our work focuses on proportion, spatial flow, materiality and long-term liveability, creating homes that feel refined, intuitive and enduring over time.

A Collaborative Partnership

Building or renovating a home is a significant undertaking requiring thoughtful coordination across many moving parts. We remain actively involved throughout design and execution, working closely with consultants, engineers, builders and suppliers to ensure the integrity of the project is maintained through to completion.
