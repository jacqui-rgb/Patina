About the Studio

A practice dedicated to creating functional, perfectly proportioned, and long-term liveable homes designed with rigorous attention to detail.

Founder & Principal

Jacqui Hurwitz

"True luxury is invisible. It’s the ease of a perfectly proportioned room, the seamless transition between spaces, and the quiet joy of a home designed around the rhythms of daily life."

Before founding Patina Design Studio, Jacqui Hurwitz spent over a decade shaping luxury residential and hospitality environments across Australia, London, Miami, and Toronto. This international perspective informs her rigorous approach to spatial planning, detailing, and materials.

With a deep belief that interior architecture directly shapes human experience, Jacqui leads every project from early schematic planning through to the fine details of custom joinery and final styling. Her approach combines European-inspired warmth with a highly practical sensibility, ensuring homes are not only beautiful but structurally resolved and exceptionally functional.

Our Philosophy

Highly Resolved. Thoughtfully Lived.

A Commitment to Depth

We intentionally limit our intake to a select number of projects each year. This is not just a business decision; it is a commitment to depth. It allows us to be highly involved at every stage, partnering closely with clients, architects, builders, and artisans from the first sketch to the final styling.

Function, Proportion, & Longevity

We believe successful design prioritizes how a space functions and feels over temporary trends. Our work centers on proportion, spatial flow, and structural design. We select high-quality materials and design bespoke elements that patinate beautifully over time, securing your investment and enhancing everyday liveability.

A Collaborative Partnership

The process of building or renovating a home is a significant journey. We act as your trusted advocate, coordinating with consultants, engineers, and builders to resolve complex details on-site. By protecting the integrity of the design through to handover, we ensure the final home is realized exactly as intended.
