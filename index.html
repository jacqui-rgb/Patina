The Studio

We believe the success of a home is determined long before finishes and furnishings are selected.

Spatial planning is one of the most important and often overlooked aspects of residential design. Patina Design Studio is frequently engaged to review, refine and improve floor plans, helping clients identify opportunities that influence how a home functions long before details become fixed.

Through years of reviewing proposed layouts for new builds, renovations and off-plan properties, the studio has developed a reputation for identifying opportunities, resolving spatial challenges and improving the way homes are experienced every day.

OUR SERVICES

The appropriate level of design involvement depends on the property, the project stage and the decisions still to be resolved.

Full Service Design

For substantial residential projects requiring interior architecture, spatial planning, furnishing and design management from early planning through completion.

Furnishing & Procurement

For homes or defined areas requiring furniture, lighting, rugs, artwork, custom pieces, procurement, installation and final placement.

Pre-Purchase Design Review

For clients seeking design insight before committing to a property, including its spatial potential, limitations and likely implications.

Layout Assessment

For existing plans that need closer review before key decisions are fixed, with attention to function, flow, proportion and long-term liveability.

Design Positioning Strategy

For properties being prepared for sale, development or market positioning, where design direction needs to support the intended buyer.

Design Consultation

For clients who require a considered starting point before the appropriate scope, service pathway and next steps are confirmed.
