Patina Design Studio identifies the spatial and interior decisions that will shape how a home works, feels and is experienced, then carries one connected design direction through planning, documentation, construction, furnishing and final placement.

Designed from the inside

The success of a home is established before finishes and furniture are selected. Floor plans, room relationships, circulation, storage, lighting, joinery and furniture placement need to be understood together while there is still time to improve them.

Patina Design Studio brings focused interior spatial judgement to renovations, new builds and properties designed by others, refining the planning and carrying the interior direction through the detailed decisions that shape the completed home.

The Studio

Patina Design Studio is a Sydney interior architecture studio specialising in substantial residential projects, complete furnishing and independent design reviews.

Led by founder and principal Jacqui Hurwitz, the studio examines how the interior will work at every scale, from the relationship between rooms to joinery, lighting, material transitions and furniture placement. Plans are not treated as fixed simply because they have already been drawn.

Early involvement allows Patina Design Studio to strengthen the planning and coordinate the decisions that will shape the completed home before approvals, documentation, budgets or construction limit the opportunity for change. The same interior direction is then carried through the project rather than being divided between separate stages and contributors.

OUR SERVICES

Services are structured around the decisions that need to be made, the stage at which those decisions can still be influenced and the level of continuity required to protect the final outcome.

Full Service Design

Substantial renovations and new builds are shaped from early planning through to handover, with spatial planning, documentation, site decisions, furnishing, installation and final placement developed with cohesive direction. The project feels connected from the larger planning decisions through to the final pieces.

Furnishing & Procurement

Furniture, lighting, rugs, artwork and custom pieces are planned, selected, procured and installed in relation to the architecture, room proportions and the way each area is used. The furnished areas feel settled, comfortable, well scaled and ready to live in from installation.

Pre-Purchase Design Review

Before a property is purchased, Patina Design Studio assesses whether its layout, light, storage, proportions and renovation potential can support the buyer’s requirements. The review looks beyond presentation to identify both opportunity and limitation before commitment.

Layout Assessment

An existing floor plan may satisfy approvals or construction requirements without fully supporting the people who will live there. Patina Design Studio independently reviews flow, room relationships, furniture planning, storage and daily use before the plan is relied on.

Design Positioning Strategy

Properties being renovated, built or developed for sale need design decisions shaped around the intended buyer rather than broad assumptions about the market. The focus is on layout, finishes, furnishing direction and presentation choices that support buyer appeal and perceived value. The same approach may also be applied to selected rental developments where the intended tenant materially affects the design direction.

Design Consultation

Before a larger scope or design work begins, Patina Design Studio takes time to understand the property, priorities and brief. The consultation provides the context to identify the right service pathway, scope, fee estimate and next steps.

Considering a Project?

Share your property, project stage, timing and requirements through the enquiry form. You can book a Design Consultation directly or ask Patina Design Studio to contact you first. The consultation reviews the property, identifies the key design considerations and defines the most appropriate way forward.
