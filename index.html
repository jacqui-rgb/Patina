The Studio

INTERIOR ARCHITECTURE AND DESIGN FOR HIGHLY RESOLVED RESIDENTIAL PROJECTS.

Patina Design Studio works closely with clients from the early planning stages through to final installation, remaining highly involved throughout the entire process.

The studio’s role extends beyond selecting finishes or creating concepts, with careful attention given to spatial planning, detailing and the decisions that influence how a home will function day to day.

Before founding Patina Design Studio, Jacqui Hurwitz worked across Australia, London, Miami and Toronto on luxury residential and hospitality projects.

While primarily focused on high-end residential projects, the studio also undertakes select hospitality and boutique commercial environments.

Patina Design Studio undertakes a select number of projects each year, allowing each project to receive a highly involved and considered approach from beginning to end.
