The Studio

We believe the success of a home is determined long before finishes and furnishings are selected.

Spatial planning is one of the most important and often overlooked aspects of residential design. Patina Design Studio is frequently engaged to review, refine and improve floor plans, helping clients identify opportunities that influence how a home functions long before details become fixed.

Through years of reviewing proposed layouts for new builds, renovations and off-plan properties, the studio has developed a reputation for identifying opportunities, resolving spatial challenges and improving the way homes are experienced every day.
