OUR SERVICES

Design Consultation

The foundational first step for all Patina Design Studio services, providing professional guidance and strategic direction.

The Service

Every beautiful, highly resolved home begins with a conversation. Our Design Consultation is a highly intensive 90-minute session to unlock your home's true potential.

Starting a major renovation, custom build, or complete furnishing project can feel incredibly overwhelming. With thousands of decisions to make, it's easy to experience analysis paralysis or make early mistakes that are difficult to correct later. This session is designed to give you immediate, high-level professional feedback and clear strategic direction.

We sit down with you—on-site or in our design studio—to review your architectural layouts, analyze your functional challenges, review your inspiration images, and establish an aesthetic and practical roadmap for future phases of design.

Who It's For & Outcome

Ideal for: Homeowners at the absolute start of their building, renovation, or furnishing journey who want professional guidance, design clarity, and a realistic path forward before committing to fees or contracts.

The Outcome: Complete clarity, design reassurance, a defined project brief, and a structured, transparent fee proposal outlining exactly how we can partner to realize your project.

What's Included

  • 90-minute intensive meeting (on-site or studio-based)
  • Detailed review of architectural layouts and floor plans
  • Analysis of spatial layout, storage, and circulation challenges
  • Guidance on aligning style inspiration with practical outcomes
  • Strategic advisory regarding Council, DA, or CDC planning approvals
  • Immediate, high-level material and color suggestions
  • Formulation of a highly structured project design brief
  • A detailed fee estimate and timeline roadmap for future design phases

Our Approach

A professional, highly personalized introduction to the design process.

01

Project Inquiry

You fill out our project enquiries form, providing details regarding location, property type, estimated timeline, and design aspirations.

02

Preparation

Our team schedules the meeting and reviews any material you share—floor plans, real estate listings, or Pinterest inspiration boards.

03

Deep-Dive Session

We meet for 90 minutes. We walk the property, analyze plans, brain-storm spatial corrections, and discuss materials and layout possibilities.

04

Strategic Brief

We draft a summary defining your project goals, functional needs, scope of works, and target budget scales to ensure we are fully aligned.

05

Design Roadmap

We deliver a comprehensive fee proposal and a visual timeline roadmap showing exactly how we will guide your project from concept to completion.
