OUR SERVICES

Design Consultation

Every project begins with understanding. Before recommending services, preparing proposals or defining scope, we take the time to understand your property, objectives and priorities to determine the most appropriate path forward.

Our Approach

Every project starts from a different place.

Some clients arrive with completed plans. Others are considering a renovation, evaluating a property purchase or exploring what may be possible within their home. Some have a clear direction, while others are seeking clarity around where to begin.

Before preparing proposals or recommending services, we take the time to understand how you live, what matters most to you and what you are hoping to achieve from the project. We review relevant plans, imagery, project information and existing conditions to establish a clear understanding of the opportunity, project scope and desired outcome.

Following the consultation, we prepare a tailored design proposal outlining the recommended service, project brief, proposed scope of works and design fee estimate.

Whether a client chooses to proceed with Patina Design Studio or independently, the consultation is intended to provide greater clarity, confidence and direction moving forward.

What's Included

  • Up to two hours of dedicated consultation time
  • Detailed discussion tailored to your property, objectives and priorities
  • Review of plans, inspiration imagery and existing conditions
  • Professional advice and strategic project guidance
  • Identification of opportunities, constraints and key project considerations
  • Comprehensive design brief and proposed scope of works
  • Design fee estimate and next-step recommendations

Who It's For

Homeowners, property purchasers and developers seeking clarity before committing to significant design, construction or furnishing decisions.

Whether you are planning a renovation, new build, furnishing project or property purchase, the consultation provides an independent assessment of the opportunity and a clear recommendation on the most appropriate next steps.

Consultation Fee

The consultation fee is $1,200 and includes up to two hours of consultation time, a detailed project brief, proposed scope of works, fee estimate and tailored next steps

Additional travel fees may apply for projects located more than 15km from our Dover Heights office.
