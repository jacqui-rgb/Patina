OUR SERVICES

Design Consultation

Planning changes to a property involves decisions about how the space will work, what should be retained and what may need to change.

The Design Consultation is the first stage of working with Patina Design Studio. We take the time to understand the property, your priorities and what you are considering, so that our recommendations for the project are informed by both the existing conditions and how you intend to use the space.

This can be particularly useful before purchasing a property, undertaking a renovation or progressing the design of a new home.

Our Approach

We review the property in relation to your requirements, considering how the layout, proportions, circulation and existing conditions affect its suitability for what you have planned.

The discussion also gives us an opportunity to understand your expectations, identify matters requiring further investigation and consider the extent of design involvement appropriate for the project.

Plans and project information are reviewed where available. You do not need to have an established brief before approaching us.

What happens next

Following the consultation, we prepare a tailored project brief and proposal outlining our understanding of your requirements, the recommended design service, proposed scope of work, design fee estimate and next steps.

This establishes how Patina Design Studio would be involved and gives you a clear basis for considering the next stage of your project.

The Design Consultation fee is $1,200 including GST. This includes up to two hours of consultation with Patina Design Studio and the written project brief and proposal outlined above.

The standard fee applies to consultations within 15 km of our Dover Heights office. For properties outside this area, any additional travel fee will be confirmed after booking and must be paid before the consultation.

What’s Included

  • Up to two hours of consultation with Patina Design Studio
  • Review of the property, existing conditions and available plans
  • Discussion of your requirements, priorities and how you intend to use the property
  • Review of relevant spatial, functional and interior design considerations
  • Identification of opportunities, constraints and matters requiring further investigation
  • Tailored project brief and recommended scope of design services
  • Design fee estimate for subsequent stages and recommended next steps

The Design Consultation is intended to provide clarity around direction, scope and next steps. It does not include design concepts, drawings, sourcing, procurement, joinery design or documentation.
