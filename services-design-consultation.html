OUR SERVICES

Design Consultation

The Design Consultation is the first formal stage of the Patina Design Studio process. It allows us to understand the property, client, priorities, brief and constraints before recommending a service, preparing a proposal or defining the scope of work.

Our Approach

Every project starts from a different point.

Clients approach Patina Design Studio at different stages. Some have plans under development, some are preparing for a renovation or new build, some are considering a property purchase and others need to complete a home through furnishing.

The consultation allows us to examine what has already been decided, identify the questions requiring attention and understand how the property needs to support the client. Available plans, photographs, project information and existing conditions are reviewed in relation to the likely scope and project stage.

The consultation is not an informal introductory call and does not provide a design concept. It establishes the professional basis from which Patina Design Studio can recommend the appropriate level of involvement and prepare a meaningful proposal.

Best suited to

Homeowners, property purchasers and developers considering a renovation, new build, furnishing project, property purchase or design review who require informed direction before progressing further.

What happens next

Following the consultation, we prepare a tailored proposal outlining the project brief, recommended service, proposed scope of work and design fee estimate. This establishes how Patina Design Studio would be involved before the next stage begins.

The consultation fee is $1,200 inc GST and includes up to two hours of consultation time, a detailed project brief, proposed scope of works, fee estimate and tailored next steps.

A travel fee may apply for consultations more than 15km from our Dover Heights office.

What’s Included

  • Up to two hours of consultation time with Jacqui Hurwitz
  • Review of the property, available plans and existing conditions
  • Discussion of client requirements, priorities, routines and constraints
  • Identification of key spatial and interior design considerations
  • Assessment of the likely scope and required level of involvement
  • Detailed project brief
  • Recommended Patina Design Studio service and proposed scope of work
  • Design fee estimate and next-step recommendations

The Design Consultation is intended to provide clarity around direction, scope and next steps. It does not include design concepts, drawings, sourcing, procurement, joinery design or documentation.
