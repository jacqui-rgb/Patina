Newcastle Waterfront

A comprehensive, high-end waterfront residential renovation optimizing spatial layouts and introducing highly-detailed bespoke interior architecture.

The Design Story

Originally engaged by the client for furnishing and styling alone, our scope quickly expanded into a complete interior architecture and Full Service Design commission.

Understanding the true potential of this waterfront location, we collaborated closely with the clients to completely re-evaluate the interior layout. The floor plan was optimized to maximize natural light and capture expansive water views while solving the typical spatial challenges of a narrow residence. We re-engineered the wall and ceiling configurations to widen corridors and create cohesive, flowing sightlines.

Every piece of joinery has been designed completely from scratch, including built-in entertainment bars, bespoke media fireplaces, detailed wardrobe systems, and custom bathroom vanities. Carefully selected finishes combine durable coastal practicality with under-stated, sophisticated luxury, utilizing a scheme of textured stones, warm timbers, and tactile wallpapers.

Construction & Site Management

Active Site Representation: As the project is currently in the active construction phase, the studio is highly involved in site coordination. We regularly attend site meetings, resolve design details in real-time, and coordinate closely with builders, joiners, and stone masons to review complex shop drawings and guarantee exceptional craftsmanship.
