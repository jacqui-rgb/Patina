SELECTED PROJECT

Vaucluse Residence

A premium, highly-considered renovation blending sophisticated aesthetics with relaxed, casual family living in Vaucluse, Sydney.

The Design Story

Initially engaged for furnishing and styling only, Patina Design Studio's scope rapidly evolved into a comprehensive, full-scale renovation of this elegant Sydney home.

The client's main objective was to transform their residence into a warm, light-filled environment suitable for a young, active family. It was essential to create functional spaces that met the practical needs of daily family life without sacrificing design integrity, sophisticated finishes, or a mature aesthetic. The challenge lay in striking the perfect balance between high-end elegance and laid-back comfort.

We responded with a design centered on natural warmth, textural layering, and flexible zones. The layout was optimized to draw natural light deep into the living spaces, showcasing the clients' remarkable and personal collection of travel-inspired art. Every detail, from custom joinery to bespoke lighting selections, was curated to complement the architectural shell and create a cohesive, inviting, and liveable masterpiece.

Design Execution & Logistics

Logistics Coordination: To ensure a completely seamless and stress-free move-in day, the studio oversaw the entire move-in logistics. We managed installers, specialized packers, removalists, professional cleaners, and unpackers, culminating in a hand-over where even the pantry and fridge were fully stocked.

The Result: A beautifully balanced, highly customized home where relaxed comfort and high-end design live in perfect harmony.
