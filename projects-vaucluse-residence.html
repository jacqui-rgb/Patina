SELECTED PROJECT

Vaucluse Residence

A layered residential renovation designed around warmth, flexibility and the practical rhythms of family living in Vaucluse, Sydney.

The Design Story

Initially engaged for furnishing, the project expanded into a broader residential renovation after early consultations revealed opportunities to improve the home’s layout, functionality and overall liveability.

Designed for a young family, the brief centred around creating a home that felt calm, spatially cohesive and adaptable to daily life without leaning overly formal or trend-driven. Spatial planning focused on improving circulation, connection between the main living areas and long-term flexibility as the family grows.

Natural light, warmth and materiality became central to the design direction, alongside a layered approach to interior architecture, custom detailing, furnishings and artwork. The result is a home that feels calm, connected and thoughtfully detailed, balancing refined detailing with the practical realities of daily family life.
