Rose Bay Penthouse

A premium off-plan penthouse redesign and spatial optimization delivering absolute customization, high-end joinery, and global art curation.

The Design Story

Initially engaged for a pre-purchase Layout Assessment, our role quickly evolved into Client Representatives on Site and Full Service Design leads for this exquisite penthouse.

Buying off-plan often means accepting developers' generic layouts. Our client wanted a home customized specifically to their daily lifestyle and entertaining needs. Patina Design Studio intervened to negotiate and implement structural layout alterations with the developers before construction locked them out. We expanded spatial thresholds, improved lighting flows, and maximized structural margins.

Beyond spatial reconfiguration, we reselected finishes, completely redesigned the kitchens and bespoke joinery packages, and sourced unique, luxury decorative lighting. Our scope also extends to curation and procurement of a complete furniture and art collection, pulling from a carefully nurtured network of local and international suppliers, artisans, and makers to select items that complement the penthouse's architectural prestige.
