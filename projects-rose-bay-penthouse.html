Rose Bay Penthouse

A premium off-plan penthouse redesign and spatial optimisation delivering absolute customisation, high-end joinery, and global art curation.

The Design Story

Initially engaged for a pre-purchase Layout Assessment, Patina Design Studio’s role quickly expanded into a broader Full Service Design scope for this off-plan Rose Bay penthouse.

Buying off-plan often means accepting generic developers’ layouts that fail to reflect how people actually live. Working closely with the clients, Patina Design Studio reworked the floor plan to better support their day-to-day routines, entertaining requirements and long-term liveability, implementing key spatial and structural changes before construction locked them in.

Beyond layout reconfiguration, the scope extended into finishes reselection, decorative lighting curation and a complete redesign of the kitchens and bespoke joinery throughout the residence. Each intervention was approached with a strong focus on functionality, spatial clarity and creating a more resolved architectural outcome.

Throughout construction, Patina Design Studio acted as client representative on site, coordinating closely with the builder, architect, developer, joiner and stone mason to ensure all design changes were clearly communicated and executed with consistency and precision.

The project also includes complete furniture and art curation, drawing from a carefully developed network of local and international suppliers, artisans and makers to create a highly personalised penthouse environment layered with warmth, sophistication and individuality.
