OUR SERVICES

Layout Assessment

A focused review of a proposed floor plan to improve functionality, flow, furniture planning and daily liveability before key decisions become fixed.

Our Approach

Many floor plans are developed to satisfy planning requirements, construction constraints, budgets and timelines. While a layout may technically work, that does not always mean it will function in the most intuitive or enjoyable way for the people using it.

Patina Design Studio reviews floor plans through the lens of how a home will be experienced day to day. We assess how spaces connect, how rooms are likely to be used, where furniture will sit, how storage has been considered, the sequence of movement through the home and the experience from key viewpoints.

We also consider the character of each space, assessing proportion, natural light and room relationships to understand how the environment is likely to feel when occupied.

This often includes assessing privacy, joinery opportunities, furniture planning and the ergonomics of kitchens, bathrooms, laundries and wardrobes to identify areas that may benefit from refinement before a project progresses to the design development stage.

What's Included

  • Assessment of layout, functionality and circulation
  • Review of room proportions and spatial relationships
  • Furniture planning and placement review
  • Identification of storage and joinery opportunities
  • Assessment of natural light and room orientation
  • Review against lifestyle requirements and future plans
  • Summary recommendations and next-step guidance

STRATEGIC ROLE

WHY AN INDEPENDENT REVIEW MATTERS

Architects, designers, builders and developers each bring valuable expertise to a project. At Patina Design Studio, spatial planning and functionality sit at the core of our work. We specialise in analysing how homes function beyond the floor plan, assessing circulation, room relationships, furniture planning, storage and daily usability to identify opportunities that can have a significant impact on how a home ultimately feels and performs.

FUNCTION BEFORE FINISHES

Beautiful finishes cannot compensate for a layout that is difficult to live in. Functionality should be resolved first.

IDENTIFYING MISSED OPPORTUNITIES

Small changes to room layouts, storage planning or circulation can have a significant impact on daily liveability.

Specialised Functional Review

An independent review provides a fresh perspective before important decisions become fixed.

REDUCING COSTLY CHANGES LATER

Resolving issues during planning is typically far simpler and more cost-effective than modifying completed construction.
