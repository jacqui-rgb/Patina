OUR SERVICES

Layout Assessment

A highly focused professional review of your architectural drawings to optimize daily flow, proportions, and liveability before construction begins.

The Service

A floor plan can include every element on your wishlist and still feel completely wrong when you live in it. Our Layout Assessment service provides an invaluable, independent second opinion.

Often, builders or developers design layouts for maximum speed of construction or broad, generic appeal, rather than considering how a specific family actually uses the spaces. We look closely at the invisible layers of flow: Where do doors swing? Is a wardrobe acting as an inconvenient corridor? Is there enough space to clean behind that beautiful freestanding tub? Is your bedroom's mini bar a safety hazard?

By correcting these friction points on paper, we prevent incredibly stressful, expensive, and time-consuming adjustments on site. We redline your plans and offer optimized alternative layouts, ensuring every room works with your daily routines instead of fighting against them.

Who It's For & Outcome

Ideal for: Homeowners working with an architect, builder, or buying an off-plan apartment who feel their layout is slightly compromised, and want expert spatial design reassurance before locking in construction documents.

The Outcome: A completely resolved, high-functioning layout scheme that maximizes space, optimizes natural light, ensures physical privacy, and acts as the perfect canvas for your daily life.

What's Included

  • Thorough technical analysis of proposed layout plans
  • Review of proportions, room relationships, and circulation
  • Detailed redline of spatial pain points and design clashes
  • Alternative schematic layout sketches mapping optimized flow
  • Joinery opportunity planning & built-in storage reviews
  • Evaluation of window placements, light behavior, and privacy
  • Ergonomic assessments of kitchens, laundries, and wet areas
  • One-on-one session to walk through recommendations
  • Actionable markups to easily communicate changes to your architect/builder

Our Approach

A surgical review that unlocks hidden spatial efficiency.

01

Submission

You provide your current architectural drawings, draft floor plans, or developer blueprints, along with details of your family's daily routine.

02

Spatial Audit

We perform a deep, systematic critique of the layout, looking for moisture traps, privacy breaches, door swing conflicts, and ventilation gaps.

03

Alternative Layouts

We trace over your plans to sketch optimized solutions—repositioning entries, separating wardrobes, and resolving focal points.

04

Review Meeting

We walk you through our recommended adjustments, detailing the specific "why" behind each shift and how it improves your spatial comfort.

05

Architect Hand-off

Armed with our visual redlines and structural annotations, you comfortably guide your architect or builder to update the plans with confidence.
