OUR SERVICES

Pre-Purchase Design

A professional design advisory service to help you assess, visualize, and validate a property's potential before committing to a purchase.

The Service

Deciding on a major residential purchase is one of life's most significant financial and emotional choices. Our Pre-Purchase Design service provides clear-sighted architectural advisory before you sign.

Often, properties are presented with styled interiors that mask underlying structural limitations, poor spatial flow, or moisture problems. Conversely, a house that appears dated and dark might actually possess incredible hidden potential that is easy to unlock. We bring professional spatial design eyes to review the layout, orientation, and structure of the property.

Whether you have floor plans or want us to accompany you to a private viewing, we provide unbiased, objective design feedback. We look at load-bearing walls, joinery opportunities, natural light behavior, and overall liveability so you can bid at auction or make an offer with absolute clarity.

Who It's For & Outcome

Ideal for: Serious property buyers, investors, or developers who are deciding between different properties, or want to confirm if a specific house can support their renovation or extension plans before making a binding offer.

The Outcome: Absolute confidence and objective peace of mind, armed with a clear understanding of the property's true capabilities, design challenges, and associated renovation budget ranges.

What's Included

  • Comprehensive review of property floor plans & listings
  • On-site property walkthrough with principal designer Jacqui Hurwitz
  • Analysis of spatial layout, flow, and structural possibilities
  • Assessment of natural light, ventilation, and orientation
  • Identification of major design constraints and moisture/structural traps
  • High-level brainstorming of potential layout transformations
  • Strategic advisory regarding heritage or local council overlays
  • Realistic discussion of potential renovation timelines and budget scales

Our Approach

A swift, highly strategic process to align with tight transaction timelines.

01

Intake

You share the real estate listing, architectural blueprints, or auction brochures of the property or properties you are actively analyzing.

02

Plan Analysis

Our team conducts a rigorous review of the layout drawings, evaluating structural load bearings, window positions, and spatial proportions.

03

Walkthrough

We meet on-site at the property (or conduct a virtual walkthrough if interstate) to inspect spatial thresholds, light depth, and build quality.

04

Strategy Session

We outline potential renovation schemes, detailing which walls can be removed, where kitchens/bathrooms can expand, and how flow can be resolved.

05

Decision

You proceed into bidding or negotiation armed with cold, objective facts, a clear architectural vision, and realistic renovation budget expectations.
