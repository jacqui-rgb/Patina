SELECTED PROJECT

LP Residence

An elegant residential renovation delivering "relaxed luxury" and sophisticated spaces for entertaining in a light-filled Sydney home.

The Design Story

Through our Full Service Design scope, Patina Design Studio transformed this spacious property into a deeply layered, highly resolved family sanctuary.

The core objective of the brief was to design a sophisticated home optimized for high-end entertaining while retaining the welcoming, durable comfort required for a family. We responded with a highly considered spatial and material layout. Circulation patterns were analyzed to establish natural flow, and we introduced beautiful custom coffer ceiling details that define distinct dining and living zones within the open-plan architecture.

A major focus was the dialogue between art and furniture. Textures, finishes, and high-quality materials were curated with absolute precision to complement the client's artwork collection. Custom bespoke joinery was integrated seamlessly across the living room, dining room, and private home office—introducing unexpected, tactile finishes (including leather, travertine, and dark-stained oak) that add structural depth and architectural interest.

A Culinary Hub & Artistry

The Heart of the Home: The kitchen was reconfigured to include a secondary built-in desk space, a feature gallery wall, and a luxurious custom-designed travertine and hand-stitched leather banquette. Natural light was strategically filtered to flood these active areas.

The Outcome: An incredibly refined, warm, and highly functional home where bespoke details elevate daily family routines into luxury experiences.
