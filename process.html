Our Process

A highly involved process from early planning through to completion. Each project is guided with careful planning, clear communication and close involvement throughout the process.

Phase One: PRE DESIGN

The foundation of every successful project. We start with understanding you, your goals and the opportunities within the space.

01
1 Week

Design Consultation

Understanding the property, goals and opportunities within the space.

02
2-3 Weeks

Briefing, Concept & Schematic

Developing the spatial direction, layout and overall design intent.

03
1-3 Days

Project Team Site Visit & Introductions

Collaborating with the broader project team to align the project goals from the outset.

Phase Two: DESIGN

Ideas are refined and resolved. We shape the design with intention, ensuring every detail is cohesive, considered and achievable.

04
6-12 Weeks

Design Development

Refining layouts, materials, details and selections.

05
4-8 Weeks

Documentation Package

Preparing detailed drawing packages and specification for construction.

06
6-9 Weeks

Tender & Estimation

Reviewing documentation collaboratively with builders and trades before construction begins.

Phase Three: DESIGN EXECUTION

We remain involved throughout construction, ensuring the design is realised with care and integrity.

07
Ongoing

Design Management During Construction

Maintaining involvement throughout construction to protect the integrity of the design.

08
1-2 Weeks

Installation & Client Handover

Coordinating the final layers of furniture, styling and installation.

09
Post Construction

Post Construction Follow-up

Ensuring the design is resolved as intended post construction.
