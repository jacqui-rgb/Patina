Our Process

Each project is approached with careful planning, clear communication, and close involvement from early concept through to completion.

Phase One: PRE DESIGN

The early planning phase focused on understanding the property, project direction and broader opportunities within the space.

01
1 Week

Design Consultation

Initial consultation to review the property, discuss project objectives and determine the appropriate project scope.

02
2-3 Weeks

Briefing, Concept & Schematic

Establishing the spatial direction, layout and overall design intent for the project.

03
1-3 Days

Project Team Site Visit & Introductions

Selecting and aligning the broader project team early to ensure clarity around direction, scope and execution.

Phase Two: DESIGN

The design is developed and resolved through detailed planning, material selection and technical coordination.

04
6-12 Weeks

Design Development

Refining the layout, material palette, joinery, lighting, fixtures, furnishings and overall design direction.

05
4-8 Weeks

Documentation Package

Preparing detailed documentation and specifications to support pricing, coordination and construction.

06
6-9 Weeks

Tender & Estimation

Reviewing documentation with builders and trades to align scope, pricing and construction requirements before commencement.

Phase Three: DESIGN EXECUTION

We remain closely involved during construction to protect the integrity of the design through to final completion.

07
Ongoing

Design Management During Construction

Reviewing details on-site and coordinating with builders and trades to ensure the design is executed as intended.

08
1-2 Weeks

Installation & Client Handover

Coordinating furniture installation, styling and final project handover.

09
Post Construction

Post Construction Follow-up

Assisting with final reviews, defect identification and coordination following project completion.
