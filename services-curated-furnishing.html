OUR SERVICES

Curated Furnishing

A premium, turnkey furniture procurement and styling service to resolve, elevate, and deeply personalize your home environment.

The Service

Moving into a new home or looking to completely redefine your current spaces? Our Curated Furnishing service handles the entire design, sourcing, procurement, and setup lifecycle.

We believe that furniture is not an afterthought, but a critical architectural element that defines the scale, atmosphere, and liveability of your rooms. We draw from our extensive network of global designers, boutique furniture makers, and custom workrooms to secure unique pieces that aren't accessible to the public.

Instead of managing endless shipping tracking numbers, assembling flat-pack items, and dealing with freight damage, our team acts as your single point of contact. We consolidate all pieces at our premium warehouse and execute a spectacular single-day delivery and complete layout installation.

Who It's For & Outcome

Ideal for: Clients moving into a newly built or purchased home who want high-quality, bespoke, and unified furniture, lighting, and artwork that honors the architecture, without managing complex logistical details.

The Outcome: A stunning, fully resolved, live-in-ready home that matches your taste and supports your daily lifestyle with effortless luxury.

What's Included

  • Detailed furniture spatial plans and layout drawings
  • Comprehensive furniture, lighting, and rug selection
  • Bespoke furniture, custom upholstery, and cushion design
  • Artwork sourcing, framing direction, and curation
  • Decorative lighting specifications (pendants, sconces, lamps)
  • Premium window treatments & drapery selections
  • Procurement, order tracking, and freight coordination
  • consolidated warehouse storage & quality inspections
  • Complete single-day installation and final professional styling

Our Approach

From strategic planning to a single-day installation reveal.

01

Discovery

We assess how you inhabit your rooms, study the existing architecture, set investment parameters, and outline a cohesive aesthetic theme.

02

Layouts & Curation

We present precise furniture layouts to optimize flow. We then curate options for every piece—furniture, fabrics, rugs, and decorative details.

03

Procurement

Once selections are approved, we handle all order placements, trade discounts, order tracking, and resolve any supply chain constraints.

04

Warehousing

Every piece is shipped to our consolidated warehouse. Our team inspects each shipment upon arrival to ensure everything is perfect and pristine.

05

Installation

We orchestrate white-glove transport, assemble all furniture, hang your artwork, style the shelves, and unveil your complete, refined home.
